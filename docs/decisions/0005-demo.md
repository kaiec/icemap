# 0005 A demo on GitHub Pages with MeSH and the STW

Status: accepted · Date: 2026-10-29

## Context

Eckert (2012, chapter 3) demonstrated the ICE-Map Visualization with MeSH (822 MEDLINE abstracts of 2008) and the STW
(intellectual, automatic and tagging results for 371 articles of three Elsevier journals). Those document sets are not
open. Both vocabularies, and the indexing done with them, are available today: MEDLINE records how each citation was
indexed (by people; automatically since April 2022; automatically and then reviewed), and the EconBiz API returns the
subjects of the records of ECONIS.

## Decision

- A demo page in `docs/` (next to `docs/decisions/`), served by GitHub Pages from `main`, folder `/docs`: one page,
  two examples (`?map=mesh|stw`), several views each, text in scientific style with the citation of Eckert (2012).
- MeSH: the descriptors of the current year by tree numbers; MEDLINE citations sampled per day by the date they
  entered PubMed (2021 and 2025), split by `IndexingMethod` into indexing by people (2021), automated and reviewed
  (2025).
- STW: the current version without deprecated concepts, categories above descriptors; ECONIS records of 2008–2010 and
  2023–2025 (20 evenly spaced pages of 1,000 records each), subjects matched to descriptors by label.
- The scripts that fetch and convert the data (`demo/mesh.py`, `demo/stw.py`, standard library) and the build
  (`demo/build.py`) are in the repository; the downloads stay outside it. Only counts per concept are published.
- The statistics are those of the component (decision 0001), unchanged.

## Consequences

The built files in `docs/` (about 5 MB) are committed and rebuilt by hand. The data credits (NLM; ZBW, CC BY 4.0) are
on the page.
